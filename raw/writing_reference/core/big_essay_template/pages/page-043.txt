第一步：引出例子
除 了 For example/ instance, 还 可 以 使 用 This point can be confirmed by the
example of my roommate, （这一观点可以通过我的室友的例子来证实）；To give
a common example, （举一个普遍的例子）。


第二步：使用短语写出简单句
写简单句表示原因（常用一般时态）:my roommate was addicted to the Internet.
                                        常见短语
   be interested in          对...感兴趣 be passionate about              对…有激情
   place a high priority on 对...重视          be tired of               对...厌烦
   attach importance to      对...重视         dedicate oneself to​      致力于...
   be committed to           致力于            place a high value on​    重视


第三步：加逗号和 which 引导一个非限定性定语从句表示结果；
, which made him fail the final exam.
其 中 made him... 可 以 替 换 为 brings him.../ enables him to develop.../helps him
cultivate.../allows him to acquire.../equips him with...等


第四步：另起一句换个角度，描述主语的心态/他人的看法/导致的结果。
For instance, my roommate, Kevin, was addicted to the Internet, which made him
fail the final exam. During that period, he regretted everything he had done.
例如，我的室友凯文沉溺于互联网，这让他没能通过期末考试。在那段时间，
他后悔他所做的一切。


或 For instance, my roommate, Kevin, was addicted to the Internet, which made him
become quiet and reserved. During that period, his real-life friends were becoming
fewer and fewer.
例如，我的室友凯文沉迷于互联网，这使他变得沉默寡言。在那段时间里，他
的现实生活中的朋友越来越少。
