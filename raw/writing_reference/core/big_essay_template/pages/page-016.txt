主体：年轻人 the girl/boy                           主要动作:think/ say/ do homework/ lie
                                           组合
The cartoons provide us with a thought-provoking scene: the girl in the left picture is
fully focused on her homework, thinking that she will feel relieved when she
finishes her work. Whereas in the right picture, the boy is lying on the sofa, telling
himself that he will not start until the deadline.
（转述）
...saying, “I will feel relieved when I finish my work”....
（直述）
The girl believes that early completion will bring her relief.
（更好的转述）
注：当描述图画内容（如人物对话、想法）时，强烈建议使用转述。因为人物
的对话往往十分简洁，简单的直接翻译会让文章显得很低级、不够连贯。
                                           升级
升级需要补充基本动作+环境（见前文）、修饰语（who 引导的定语从句）。


     , sitting in front of the desk,              who is lying
     结构：, 基本动作（doing）,                            结构：who 引导的定语从句



The cartoons provide us with a thought-provoking scene: the girl in the left picture,
sitting in front of the desk, wholly focuses on her homework and says that she will
feel relieved when she finishes her work. However, the boy in the right picture who
is lying on the sofa says that he will not start until the last minute.


学至此处有没有觉得很熟悉呢？你会发现两幅图和一幅图写法类似，熟练后不
必再看公式区分，直接增加句子成分、拉长句子、灵活使用就可以了。
