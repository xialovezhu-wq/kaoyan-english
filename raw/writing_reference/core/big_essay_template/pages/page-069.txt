均为万能句
待选 1：If these proposed measures are achieved, a harmonious balance between A
and B will be within our reach.（注：A、B 根据主题词来定）
如果这些提议的措施得以实现，A 和 B 之间的平衡触手可及。
待选 2：In this way, hopefully, we may have a better future.
这样我们才有希望拥有一个更加美好的未来。
待选 3：Let us believe that this is the first step toward a brighter future for all of us.
让我们相信这是我们所有人迈向更光明未来的第一步。
待选 4 ：Only through the joint efforts of all people can we effectively solve the
problem/ have a better life/ push the social progress.
只有通过所有人的共同努力，我们才能有效地解决问题/拥有更好的生活/推动
社会进步。
待 选 5 ： There is no better time than now for us to take collective and decisive
action to address this pressing issue.​   ​
现在是我们采取集体和果断行动解决这一紧迫问题的最佳时机。
