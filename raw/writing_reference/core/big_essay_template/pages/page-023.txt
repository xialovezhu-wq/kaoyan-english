                                  方案 5：强调句型
It is the 主题词 that must deserve greater attention.
以创新为例（特别注意，好多年没考的热点话题）：
The drawing, undoubtedly, highlights the core idea that it is the innovation that
must deserve greater attention.
毫无疑问，图画强调的核心观点是创新必须得到高度重视。
还可以使用：
It is the 主题词 that must be our top priority. 首要任务
It is the 主题词 that requires our immediate attention. 立即关注
至此，第一段讲解结束。以 2024 年英一为例，完整的第一段是：
     The drawing on the left side provides us with a lively scene: several urban
residents are exercising in a park. A youngster says that the new park is fantastic.
Referring to the statistics provided in the right chart, the number of parks in a certain
city had remarkably increased from 406 in 2020 to 670 in 2022. The purpose of the
drawing and the chart seem to remind us that great accomplishments have been
made in the construction of public facilities by the government.
注 1：现阶段对于第一段的学习，应先从细节跳脱出来，避免纠结，先保证能
够迅速地写出上述简单的例子，然后在此基础上扣细节。
