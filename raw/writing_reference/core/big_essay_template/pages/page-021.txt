                             方案 2：给予高度重视
Utmost importance ought to be attached to 主题词 by 某人.
注：“某人”一般指政府、机构、个人、民众等。
以保护环境为例，2011 年：




Simple as the drawing is, it conveys a truth that the utmost importance ought to be
attached to environmental protection by our government and the folks.
图画虽然简单但是蕴含真理，我们的政府和公民应当给予环境保护高度重视。
还可以换种写法——
某人 should give the highest priority to 主题词.
Simple as the drawing is, it conveys a truth that our government and citizens should
give the highest priority to environmental protection.
图画虽然简单但是蕴含真理，即我们的政府和公民应该把环境保护放在首位。
