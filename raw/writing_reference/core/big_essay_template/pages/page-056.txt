                        方案四：not only…but also…的倒装
公式：Not only does 主题词 increase 某方面 but it also enables them to 某方面.
注：increase 可根据语境替换为 bring 带来、facilitate 便利、enhance 提升等。
例 2022 年英一：
Not only does attending lectures increase undergraduates' academic knowledge,
but it also enables them to expand their horizons.
听讲座不仅能增加大学生的知识，还能开拓他们的眼界。
例 2023 年英一：
Not only does attending dragon boat races improve our physical and mental
health, but it also enables us to inherit and develop the traditional culture.
参加龙舟比赛不仅有助于身体和心理健康，还有助于传承和发展传统文化。
例 2024 年英一：
Not only does constructing parks improve people's living environment, but it also
attracts them to exercise.
建造公园不仅改善了人们的生活环境，而且还吸引人们去锻炼身体。
