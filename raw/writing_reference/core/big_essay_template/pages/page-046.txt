For example, my grandfather is passionate about watching television and reading
newspaper, which allows him to acquire current affairs.
例如，我的爷爷，对看电视和看报纸非常上瘾，这让他了解到新闻时事。
注：本方案更适用于与个人相关的作文。
                           方案二：宾语从句+定语从句
公式：
The majority of people believe that 主 题 词 will provide them with a broader
platform to 主 题 词 带 来 的 好 处 (do Sth.), which may put them in a beneficial
position in the future.
注 1：believe 可被替换为 maintain 坚持认为、hold the view 持有观点；
注 2：provide them with a broader platform 可被替换为 present them with a unique
chance/ create more opportunities for them
注 3：which may put them in a beneficial position in the future.可被替换为 which is
likely to secure a competitive edge in the future.
