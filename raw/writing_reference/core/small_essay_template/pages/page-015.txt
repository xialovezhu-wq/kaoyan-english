                                   能力展示和要求
28. Over the past few years, I have consistently excelled in my role, which is
      evidenced by the awards and recognition I've received.​
      我在工作中一直表现出色，所获得的奖项和认可就是证明。
29. I am a highly self-motivated and resilient individual, capable of maintaining
      focus and productivity in fast-paced environments.
      我是一个高度自律且坚韧的人，能够在快节奏的环境中保持专注和效率。
30. I thrive in collaborative settings and have often been the one to facilitate
      communication and integrate diverse viewpoints in the team.
      我在协作环境中能充分发挥能力，并经常在团队中充当促进沟通、整合不
      同观点的角色。
31. The selected candidates will assist in conducting surveys, collecting and
      analyzing data of____. Moreover, the candidates should be enthusiastic about
      ____ and have sound organization and communication skills.
      被选中的候选人将协助进行 XX 的调查、收集和分析数据。此外，候选人
      应对 XX 充满热情，并具有良好的组织和沟通能力。
32. The role requires the ability to analyze complex problems, develop practical
      solutions, and execute them effectively.
      该职位要求具备分析复杂问题、制定切实可行方案并有效执行的能力。
33. The candidates should be enthusiastic about 某 事                 and have sound
      organization/ communication/ teaching/ planning skills.
      候选人应该对某事有热情，并且拥有良好的组织/沟通/教学/策划能力。
