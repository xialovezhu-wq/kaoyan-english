First and foremost, the singing contest is to be held on the playground at 7:00 p.m.

例 2023 年英语二写给 David 关于看展的建议信：




What makes the art exhibition highly recommendable is the unique experience it
offers, which includes an art performing segment. What’s more, the art exhibition
provides a unique opportunity to let you explore yourself with creativity. Finally, it
allows you to engage with different cultures and social customs.
之所以极力推荐这个艺术展，是因为它提供了独特的观展体验，其中包含艺术
表演环节。更重要的是，艺术展览提供了一个独特的机会，让你用创造力探索
自己。最后，它可以让你接触到不同的文化和社会习俗。
