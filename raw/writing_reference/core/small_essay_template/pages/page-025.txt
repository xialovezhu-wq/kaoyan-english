                          第三段：表示情感+期待回信
                                第一句：表示情感
即表示感谢、歉意、致敬、问好等。
I would like to express my gratitude for your kind consideration of my
suggestion/application.
我想对您考虑我的建议/申请表示感谢。
I am grateful for the opportunity to submit my application/suggestion for your
consideration and look forward to the possibility of contributing.
我很感激有机会提交我的申请/建议供您考虑，并期待有可能做出贡献。
Please accept my sincere apology for any inconveniences that I have caused.
给您带来的不便，请接受我诚挚的歉意。
I hope all the members would enjoy the journey.
我希望所有的成员都能享受这段旅程。
For more information about the activity, please do not hesitate to contact me.
如需了解更多关于此书/电影/旅程的信息，请随时与我联系。
We would appreciate it very much if you could accept our invitation.
如果您能接受我们的邀请，我们将不胜感激。
We hope to see your wholehearted participation and make this event/activity a
grand success.
我们希望看到你的全身心参与，使这次活动取得圆满成功。
It would be our great honor if you could grace the occasion with your presence.
如若您能莅临，我们将倍感荣幸。
以下问候家人可以通用
Please give my best regards to your family.
Could you offer my best wishes to your family?
Kindly send my sincere regards to your family.
